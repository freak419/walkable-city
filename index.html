Walkable City

A small mountain town at sunset. Every building is a project in a portfolio, shaped like what it does: a cabin, a saucer, a chapel, a market, a venue, a gate, a clinic, a barn, an arcade. Cross the bridge, walk the square, wander the lanes; nothing is more than a minute away. Fork it, swap in your own projects, ship it.
